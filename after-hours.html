Meetings After Hours

Your legal matters don't wait for 9-to-5. We offer evening and weekend appointments by request — because life happens outside business hours.

Flexible Scheduling

We know work, caregiving, and life commitments don't always align with traditional office hours. That's why we offer appointments early mornings, evenings, and weekends by arrangement. Just let us know what works for you.

Request an after-hours appointment

What We Can Handle After Hours

Initial consultations — Discuss your legal matter at a time that suits you.

Document review & signing — Wills, powers of attorney, real estate documents, separation agreements.

Follow-up meetings — Check in on your file progress without taking time off work.

Urgent matters — Time-sensitive issues that can't wait until the next business day.

Virtual Options Available

Prefer not to travel? We offer secure video conferencing for after-hours meetings. All you need is a device with a camera and internet connection. We'll send a secure link — no software to install.

In-person after-hours meetings are held at our Ile des Chenes office (101-430 Main Street) by appointment only.

How to Book

1. Contact us — Call (431) 244-6800 or email inquire@lglegal.ca.

2. Request after-hours — Let us know your preferred days/times.

3. We confirm — We'll match you with an available lawyer and confirm the appointment.

4. Meet — In-person at our office or via secure video link.
