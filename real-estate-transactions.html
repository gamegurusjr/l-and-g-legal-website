About Real Estate Transactions

Buying or selling property is likely the largest financial transaction you'll make. We handle the legal work so the deal closes cleanly — title searches, mortgage registration, adjustments, and the keys in your hand on closing day.

We work with:

  • Buyers & sellers — Residential, recreational, commercial
  • Realtors & brokers — Smooth coordination, fast turnaround
  • Lenders — Mortgage instructions, registration
  • Refinancing & equity take-outs

Erik Gusdal runs our real estate practice from the Ile des Chenes office. He's the point of contact for realtors, lenders, and clients — responsive, thorough, and familiar with the southeastern Manitoba market. Deliver documents to 101-430 Main St., Box 629, Ile des Chenes, R0A 0T0, or email realestate@lglegal.ca.
