At-Your-Door Services

We bring our legal expertise to you — at home, in hospital, or at a care facility. No travel, no hassle, same trusted guidance.

How It Works

We understand that visiting a law office isn't always possible. Whether you're recovering from surgery, caring for a loved one, or simply unable to travel, our lawyers will come to you. We handle the paperwork, witness signatures, and provide the same thorough counsel you'd receive in our office.

Request a visit

Services Available At Your Door

Wills & Powers of Attorney — Execute your estate documents in the comfort of your home.

Real Estate Closings — We can meet at your kitchen table to review and sign closing documents.

Family Law Matters — Discuss separation agreements, parenting plans, and more privately.

Corporate Documents — Annual resolutions, share transfers, and other business matters.

Our Coverage Area

We serve clients throughout Ile des Chenes and surrounding communities in southeastern Manitoba. If you're outside our immediate area, contact us — we'll do our best to accommodate.

Travel fees may apply for locations beyond 30km from our office. We'll confirm any costs before scheduling your visit.

What to Expect

1. Book a visit — Call or email us to schedule a convenient time.

2. We prepare — We'll gather all necessary documents and information in advance.

3. We come to you — A lawyer arrives at the agreed time and location.

4. Sign & complete — Documents are witnessed, signed, and you receive copies immediately.
