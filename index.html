Working with us

Three steps to get a file started, from first contact to your first conversation with a lawyer.

01

Book a consultation

Send us your contact details and someone from our office will follow up to discuss your matter. We can't commit to acting until we've checked for conflicts and confirmed we're the right fit — but we'll always get back to you.

02

Get a fee estimate

Ask what a matter like yours typically costs. Every file is unique, but knowing the general range up front makes it easier to plan and proceed with confidence.

03

Referral credit

Clients referred to us by a realtor, lender, mortgage broker, or someone we've worked with before can receive up to $50 off their final legal fee. Just let us know who sent you.
